# Tesla Model Y vs BYD Atto 3 Comparison

## Tesla Model Y
1. **Price & Warranty**: The Tesla Model Y has varying price points depending on the chosen model, with a basic warranty of 4 years or 50,000 miles and an 8-year warranty for the battery and drive unit (100,000 miles).
2. **Range**: The estimated range is up to 321 miles on a full charge, allowing for longer trips without frequent recharging.
3. **Charging Options**: The Model Y supports Supercharging, with a peak charging rate of 225 kW, capable of adding up to 160 miles of range in just 15 minutes.

## BYD Atto 3
1. **Price and Variants**: The BYD Atto 3 has a starting price of approximately £30,000, with pricing varying based on the trim and features chosen. It offers two battery options: a Standard Range with 49.9 kWh and an Extended Range with 60.5 kWh.
2. **Range**: The Atto 3 provides a WLTP range of up to 420 km for the Extended Range variant and around 340 km for the Standard Range version, making it suitable for both urban and longer-distance travels.
3. **Charging Options**: The Atto 3 supports AC charging with a speed of 7.2 kW, taking about 9.5 to 10 hours for a full charge. It also features DC fast charging (capped at 80 kW), allowing users to recharge from 30 to 80% in roughly 36 minutes.

## Recommendation
Both vehicles present strong options for a sales fleet. The **Tesla Model Y** offers superior range and fast charging capabilities, making it suitable for longer journeys. In contrast, the **BYD Atto 3** is more budget-friendly while providing ample range for urban use. Depending on the budgetary constraints and requirements for vehicle range, I recommend considering the **Tesla Model Y** for its extended range and performance, particularly if longer distances between charging stations are anticipated. For a more cost-effective solution, the **BYD Atto 3** is a commendable alternative.
