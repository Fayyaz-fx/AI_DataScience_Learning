# Public EV Charging Landscape in the US

## Number of Public Charging Points

As of July 2026, there are over **242,000 public EV charging ports** available across the United States. This marks an increase of **34.6%** from previous counts and demonstrates significant progress in the expansion of EV infrastructure. Of these, approximately **175,675** ports are AC Level 2 charging stations, which are commonly used for everyday charging needs.

## Major Charging Networks

### 1. ChargePoint
ChargePoint is one of the largest and most prevalent charging networks in the United States, boasting a robust infrastructure that supports thousands of charging stations nationwide. The network offers a user-friendly app that provides real-time information on charging station availability, facilitating easier planning for users and fleets alike. Additionally, ChargePoint stations can also integrate with various fleet management systems to optimize routes and charging times.

### 2. Electrify America
Electrify America operates a wide network of fast-charging stations strategically located along major highways and in urban areas, making it ideal for long-distance travel and fleet operations. The network supports ultra-fast charging capabilities which can significantly reduce downtime, vital for maintaining efficiency in fleet use. Their wide array of charging options ensures compatibility with different EV models, making it a flexible choice for diverse fleet needs.
