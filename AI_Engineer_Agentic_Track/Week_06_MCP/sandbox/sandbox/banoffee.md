# Banoffee Pie Recipe

## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 cups graham cracker crumbs
- 1/2 cup butter, melted
- 1 can (14 ounces) sweetened condensed milk (for dulce de leche)
- 3 ripe bananas, sliced
- 1 1/2 cups heavy whipping cream
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- Grated chocolate or cocoa powder for topping

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the Crust:**
   - Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
   - In a medium bowl, combine graham cracker crumbs and melted butter.
   - Press the mixture into the bottom and up the sides of a pie plate. Bake for 8-10 minutes, or until golden. Let cool completely.

2. **Make Dulce de Leche:**
   - Place the sweetened condensed milk can in a pot of simmering water and cook for 2 hours. Ensure the can is always submerged to prevent it from exploding.
   - After 2 hours, let it cool before opening.

3. **Layer the Pie:**
   - Spread the dulce de leche over the cooled crust.
   - Arrange the sliced bananas on top of the dulce de leche.

4. **Prepare the Whipped Cream:**
   - In a large bowl, beat heavy whipping cream with sugar until soft peaks form.
   - Spread the whipped cream over the bananas.

5. **Garnish:**
   - Sprinkle with grated chocolate or cocoa powder on top.
   - Chill in the refrigerator until serving.

Enjoy your delicious Banoffee Pie!
